Wilbert Drive Sync

Wilbert Drive Sync is a privately operated set of workflows used to maintain the owner's websites, files, reporting, and connected media channels. Its Google OAuth integration allows the owner to authorize selected Google services for those workflows.

Privacy policyTerms of service

What the app does

With the account owner's permission, Wilbert integrations may read connected Google information such as authorized YouTube channel and playlist reporting, or support file and reporting workflows associated with the owner's Google account. Functionality is limited by the permissions granted and the integration being used.

These tools are for authorized owner operations, not a public social network, advertising platform, or third-party data marketplace.

Who operates it?

Wilbert is maintained for private use by the owner of this website and related projects. It is not a Google product and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.

Questions about access, deletion, or the application can be sent to william@gayhart.org.
